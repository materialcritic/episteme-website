title: "Submit to Episteme"
---
Episteme publishes essays, commentary, interviews and book reviews by students of the Department of Political Science. If you have something to say, we would like to read it.

## What we look for

Original, well-argued writing on politics, society, law, international relations, political theory or public life. Your piece should take a position, support it with evidence, and be readable by someone outside your sub-field.

## Submission guidelines

- **Length:** 3,000 to 4,000 words, excluding the reference list.
- **Citations:** APA style (7th edition), with in-text citations and a full reference list at the end.
- **Language:** English.
- **Originality:** the piece must be your own, unpublished work. Plagiarism, including unattributed use of AI tools or other writers' text, leads to rejection.
- **Format:** a Word document (.docx) or a Google Doc shared with view access. Use a clear title and headings where they help the reader.
- **Images:** optional. Send them as separate files and tell us the source and who holds the rights.

## What to send

Email **editor@epistemejamia.in** with the subject line **Submission: [your title]** and include:

1. Your piece, attached as a document.
2. Your name, programme and year.
3. A two-line summary of the piece, which we show on the blog.
4. Two or three suggested tags (for example: Political Theory, Indian Politics, International Relations).
5. A short author bio of about 50 words, optional.

## What happens next

The editors read every submission. We may suggest revisions before a piece is published, and we will always agree the final version with you. We aim to reply within [number] weeks. Published pieces appear on the blog, and selected work may also appear in a print or PDF issue.

## Questions

Write to the editors at the same address. We are happy to discuss an idea before you start writing.
